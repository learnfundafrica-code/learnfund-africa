Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about LearnFund Africa

What are the requirements to apply?
You need a valid student ID, admission letter from a recognized institution, and your personal details. No collateral required!
How long does approval take?
Most applications are approved within 24-72 hours after verification with your institution.
What is the repayment period?
Repayment periods range from 30 to 180 days, depending on the service and amount. We offer flexible, student-friendly terms.
How does the Student Credit Score work?
Your on-time repayments build your credit history, which can help you access larger loans, better rates, and even job opportunities after graduation.
Can international students apply?
Currently, we serve students enrolled in African institutions. We're expanding to more countries soon!

Frequently Asked Questions

What does health insurance cover?
Outpatient visits, inpatient care, prescription drugs, dental, optical, and emergency services at partner hospitals.
Are pre-existing conditions covered?
Pre-existing conditions are covered after a 3-month waiting period. Emergency care is always covered.
How do I file a device insurance claim?
Report within 24 hours of incident via our app, provide police report for theft, and we'll process within 7 days.
Is mental health support really free?
Yes! All students get 4 free counseling sessions per year, with additional sessions at reduced rates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about tuition financing

How long does approval take?
Most applications are approved within 24-72 hours after we verify your enrollment with your institution. In some cases, it can be as fast as 24 hours.
Is collateral really not required?
Yes! We believe your education is your best collateral. No assets, guarantors, or down payments required.
What happens if I can't repay on time?
We offer flexible repayment options. Contact us immediately if you're facing difficulties - we can arrange an extended payment plan or restructuring.
Can I repay early?
Yes! Early repayment is encouraged. If you repay within 30 days, there's no interest at all. Early repayment also improves your credit score.
What if my institution isn't on the partner list?
You can still apply! We're constantly adding new institutions. We'll verify your enrollment directly with your school.
